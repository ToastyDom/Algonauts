The Algonauts Project

The Algonauts Project, first launched in 2019, is on a mission to bring biological and machine intelligence researchers together on a common platform to exchange ideas and explore the intelligence frontier.

Inspired by astronauts’ exploration of space, “algonauts” explore biological and artificial intelligence with state-of-the-art algorithmic tools, advancing both fields. Previous challenges studied brain responses to images, short videos and multimodal movies. The 2027 challenge extends this progression to active video game play.

The 2027 Challenge

From watching movies to playing games.

Video gameplay brings together perception, decision making, and action in dynamic environments.

Subjects interpret audiovisual information, influence their subsequent sensory experience through their decisions and actions, and respond to events such as encountering enemies, collecting items, or taking damage. The goal of the Algonauts Project 2027 challenge is to develop fMRI encoding models that predict brain responses during video game play and that generalize to new levels and games.

In collaboration with the Courtois NeuroMod Project (CNeuroMod), the challenge provides large scale fMRI recordings acquired while subjects played Super Mario Bros., Super Mario All-Stars, Super Mario Bros. 3 and Shinobi III. Final rankings are based on how well models generalize to previously unseen games.
